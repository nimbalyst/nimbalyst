# Launch dashboard product brief

## The idea

The launch dashboard gives a small product team one calm place to understand revenue momentum, customer growth, and the next decision that needs attention. It favors a clear story over a wall of charts: what changed, why it matters, and what the team should do next.

## Who it is for

The first version is for a team preparing a product launch. They already have monthly data, but they need a shared view that keeps planning conversations grounded in the same numbers.

## What success looks like

- A reader can understand the current trajectory in under a minute.
- The dashboard highlights both progress and the remaining gap to the launch target.
- Every headline number can be traced back to an editable project file.
- The design remains readable when embedded inside a document.

## Scope for the first launch

The first release includes four headline metrics, a six-month revenue trend, a concise monthly breakdown, and a launch-readiness note. Deeper segmentation and custom date ranges can wait until the core view is useful in a real planning conversation.
